Free and Pro

The free app is the real app, with ads. Pantry Pro is an optional monthly or yearly subscription through Google Play; the price shows in the app in your currency.

Free

  • Unlimited items, barcode scan and manual entry
  • Expiry alerts and the home-screen widget
  • Shopping list

Pro

  • No ads
  • Receipt import with on-device text recognition
  • Household sync: share one pantry with an 8-character household code

Private by design

Your pantry lives on your phone. Barcode lookups send only the barcode number to Open Food Facts. Household sync (Pro) is opt-in and shares only the pantry items with the people who have your household code.

In your language

On first launch you pick your language. The app downloads a free on-device translation pack from Google (ML Kit) for it and translates its screens on your phone; you can change the language any time in Settings. Translations are automatic, so a phrase may read a little differently than a native speaker would put it.

FAQ

Does it work offline?

Yes. Scanning, items, alerts and the shopping list all work offline. Only the product-name lookup needs a connection.

Where do product names come from?

Open Food Facts, a free public database. The app sends the barcode number and shows you the suggested name, which you can change. You can also skip the lookup.

What is household sync?

A Pro feature: start a household, share its 8-character code, and everyone with the code sees and edits the same pantry. Leave the household at any time; your local pantry stays.

Is receipt import private?

Yes. Text recognition runs on your phone and the receipt photo is not uploaded. You review every line before it is added.
